01 / Who this is for

Getting found by high-intent local and regional buyers.

When someone needs a supplier, a contractor or a service nearby, they search, and they usually contact one of the first businesses that appear. Local and regional buyers who search with a clear need are the most valuable traffic a business can receive.

This page covers SEO audits, on-page setup, monthly SEO plans, Google Business Profile setup and local SEO. Sanjeev scopes the work directly with you and reports against a baseline recorded on Day 1, so you can see what changed and what did not.

SEO works on top of a working website. If your site is not built yet, or is too broken to index, the website comes first. See website development and deployment (/services-web).
02 / Problems this can address

Zero visibility in local map packs and organic searches.

You do not appear on the map.

Searches for your service in your area show competitors in the map results and not you.

Your Google Business Profile is missing or incomplete.

It is unclaimed, unverified, lacks categories or photos, or shows wrong details.

Your site is not found for what you sell.

Pages are not indexed, titles are generic, or no page answers the question the buyer typed.

Technical problems hold the site back.

It loads slowly on a phone, is missing a sitemap, or has no structured data.

Business details are inconsistent.

Your name, address or phone number differs across directories, which weakens trust signals.

Competitors answer questions you do not.

Their pages and articles cover the topics buyers research before they enquire.

Few or no reviews.

Buyers compare options, and a listing with no recent reviews looks unproven.

Search problems are rarely one fault. The audit identifies which of these are actually holding you back and puts them in priority order, so money goes to the biggest gap first.

03 / How it works

Technical foundation, structured content and Google Maps optimization.

Technical foundation.

Search engines must be able to find, read and trust the site. That means indexing, a sitemap, robots.txt, mobile-first layout, Core Web Vitals (page speed and stability) and JSON-LD schema markup.

Structured content.

Pages are matched to what buyers actually search, with clear titles, meta descriptions, headings and internal links. Long-form articles answer the questions buyers ask before they enquire.

Google Maps optimization.

Your Google Business Profile is set up and managed with the right categories, geotagged photos, a services menu and regular posts. Directory citations and a review workflow support it.

Measurement.

Google Analytics 4 and Search Console record impressions, clicks, rankings and enquiries, so progress is measured and not guessed.

Link acquisition.

On the Growth plan, strategic link acquisition builds authority through legitimate sources, not purchased links.

Organic search compounds over months. It is slower than paid advertising, but the gains stay in place while you keep the work going. Setup alone will not sustain rankings.

06 / What you get and what is excluded

Transparent search deliverables and organic realities.

Included in the agreed scope:

  • The deliverables listed for each option in the rates table, and nothing implied beyond them.
  • A prioritized, written action list from the audit.
  • Meta tags, schema, sitemap, robots.txt and analytics setup where On-Page Setup is in your quote.
  • A Google Business Profile created or claimed, with categories, photos, services and launch posts where that setup is in your quote.
  • Articles and keyword work as stated in your monthly plan.
  • A monthly performance report measured against your Day 1 baseline.
  • Accounts for Search Console, Analytics and Google Business Profile held in your name.

Not included unless quoted separately:

  • Any guaranteed ranking position, traffic figure or enquiry count. Search results depend on competition and Google's own systems.
  • Paid advertising, including Google Ads management.
  • Purchased reviews, fake reviews or purchased links. These are against search engine rules and are not offered.
  • Website design, redesign or development. See website development and deployment (/services-web).
  • Professional photography and any information only you can supply about your services.
  • Paid directory listing fees and paid tool subscriptions, if any are required, which are quoted before they are used.
  • Work outside the keywords, areas and articles stated in the approved scope.

Organic search is not instant. Rankings move over months, and search engines change their systems without notice. These are the realities, stated up front.

09 / Pricing, billing and running costs

Monthly retainer vs one-off optimization costs.

One-off optimization:

The SEO Audit, On-Page Setup and Google Business Profile Setup are billed per project, 50% to start and 50% on delivery. They fix the foundation and are not a continuing service.

Monthly retainers:

Monthly SEO (Starter or Growth) and Local SEO are retainers billed monthly in advance. They pay for the continuing work: new content, profile management, reviews, links and reporting. The retainer length and notice terms are written into your quote.

Why setup alone is not enough:

A one-off setup can make a site findable, but rankings are held and improved by continuing work. A site left alone after setup usually loses ground as competitors keep publishing and updating.

Costs billed by others:

Domain, hosting, paid directory listings and any paid tools are billed by those providers. Paid advertising spend is separate and not part of these plans.

Netqorix itemises these in your quote so you can see what is one-off and what continues before you commit.

FAQ

SEO & local search questions.

How long before I see results?
SEO is a 3 to 6 month process, and rankings compound over time. Some improvements, such as fixing indexing, show earlier. The Day 1–180 roadmap shows what happens in each phase and how it is measured.
Can you guarantee the first position on Google?
No. No provider can guarantee a ranking position. What is committed to is the work carried out and an honest monthly report against your Day 1 baseline.
What is the difference between SEO and local SEO?
SEO helps your website appear in organic search results. Local SEO focuses on your Google Business Profile and map results for nearby searches. Many businesses need both. Local SEO matters most when customers search for a service in a specific area.
Do I need a Google Business Profile?
If you serve customers in a specific area, yes. It is how you appear in the map results. Netqorix can create or claim the listing and manage it monthly under the Local SEO plan.
Can you get me reviews?
Netqorix sets up a review collection workflow so your genuine customers are asked for reviews. Purchased or fake reviews are not offered, because they break platform rules and can get a listing removed.
Who owns the Search Console, Analytics and Google Business Profile accounts?
You do. They are set up in your name, and Netqorix is given access to work on them.
What do I need to provide?
Access to your website and accounts, accurate business details, real photos and prompt approvals. Information only you know about your services makes articles more useful. The roadmap lists what is needed in each phase.
Should I start with the audit or a monthly plan?
Usually the audit, because it shows which problems are actually holding you back and in what order to fix them. A monthly plan follows once the foundation is in place, or alongside it.
Do you work with businesses outside Navi Mumbai?
Yes. Netqorix is based in Nerul, Navi Mumbai and serves businesses across Mumbai, India and internationally. Tell us the area you want to be found in when you enquire.
Do you manage Google Ads?
Paid advertising is not part of these plans. This page covers organic search and local search only.
