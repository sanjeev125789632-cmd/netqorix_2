08 / FAQ

Before we begin.

Clear answers to the questions that usually affect scope, ownership and delivery.

Can a business website really launch in 10 days?
Yes, when the agreed scope, content and approvals are ready. The 10-day commitment applies to the defined business-website scope; larger applications, e-commerce builds and delayed client approvals require a separate delivery plan.
Who owns the website and source code?
You receive 100% ownership of the approved source code and intellectual property after final settlement. The project is handed over without platform or vendor lock-in.
How does fixed pricing work?
Netqorix confirms the pages, functions, integrations, content responsibilities and delivery milestones before work begins. The resulting quote is fixed for that scope; requested additions are priced separately before implementation.
What happens after launch?
You receive the production website, repository, credentials and handover documentation. Optional maintenance is available, but you are not required to retain Netqorix to keep using your website.
Can I inspect completed work before contacting you?
Yes. Every featured project links to its live public website, and the Work section includes detailed case studies describing the published scope and results.
What happens if my website isn't delivered in 10 days?
Not live in 10 days? You don't pay the balance.
