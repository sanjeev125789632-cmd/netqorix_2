01 / Who this is for

Why a business website still matters.

When someone hears about your business, the first thing they do is search for it. If nothing comes up, or what comes up is a half-finished social page, the enquiry usually goes to whoever does have a proper website.

A website is the one place online you actually control. It works at 11pm, it answers the same questions without getting tired, and it lets a stranger decide you are worth contacting before they ever speak to you.

This page covers business websites, larger content-managed sites, e-commerce, portals, interactive 3D websites and the deployment work that puts them live.

03 / Which kind of website you need

Six kinds of website, in plain terms.

A business website

Five to fifteen pages explaining who you are, what you offer and how to reach you. This is what most businesses need, and it is what the Starter and Growth packages cover.

A content-managed website

The same thing, plus a CMS — an admin screen where you update text, images, prices and blog posts yourself, without calling a developer. Included in Growth.

An online store

Products, a cart, checkout, payment and order management. More complex than a business site because money and stock are involved. Quoted under Custom.

A portal or web application

Customers or staff log in and do something: book, track, submit or manage records. This is software, not pages — see mobile and web app development (/services-app).

An interactive 3D website

Visitors rotate, zoom or configure a product in the browser, built with Three.js or WebGL. Priced separately below.

A landing page

A single focused page for one campaign or one offer. Usually quoted as a small Custom item rather than a package.

"If you are unsure, describe what you want a visitor to be able to do. If they only need to read and then contact you, a business website is enough. If they need to log in, pay or manage something, you are looking at an application."

06 / Interactive 3D websites

When the website itself is the product demo.

An interactive 3D website lets a visitor rotate a product, look inside it, or change its colour and configuration directly in the browser. It is built with Three.js or WebGL and runs on an ordinary web page — no app to install, no plugin.

Essential:

One or two 3D scenes: an animated 3D hero, or a single product viewer visitors can rotate and zoom, with scroll-driven effects. Suitable when you have one flagship product worth showing properly.

Immersive:

A full 3D experience with custom models, physics and interaction, and a product configurator where choices change what is displayed and what is quoted. Suitable when the product genuinely has to be explored to be understood.

"The 3D models themselves are a separate deliverable. If you do not already own suitable models, they are produced under 3D modeling and visualization (/services-3d) and quoted as their own line items. A model built for a printed catalogue will not perform in a browser without being optimised for it, so the end use is confirmed before either piece is quoted."

Interactive 3D adds real cost and real weight to a page. It earns its place for products where configuration or internal detail drives the sale. For a standard services business, a well-built Growth site will do more for enquiries.

07 / Deployment: putting it live

The part between "finished" and "live".

Deployment is the work of getting a finished website onto the internet at your own domain, securely and repeatably. It is quoted as a one-time item at ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 per project and covers:

  • Domain pointing. Connecting your domain name so visitors reach your site.
  • Cloud hosting configuration. Setting up where the site actually runs.
  • SSL installation. The padlock in the browser bar. Without it, browsers warn visitors that the site is not secure.
  • A CI/CD pipeline. An automated path from approved change to live site, so updates are repeatable rather than files dragged onto a server by hand.
  • Go-live audit. Checking that pages load, forms deliver, links work, analytics record, and search engines can read the site before it is announced.

The domain registration fee and the hosting or cloud provider's charges are billed by those providers, not by Netqorix. For larger infrastructure, autoscaling or ongoing server administration, see cloud services and infrastructure (/services-cloud).

12 / Ongoing and third-party costs

Clarify what recurs before you start.

The package price is a one-time project cost. These continue afterwards and are billed by other providers:

  • Domain registration and annual renewal.
  • Web hosting or cloud provider charges.
  • Business email accounts.
  • Paid plugins, themes, fonts and licensed assets.
  • Payment gateway transaction fees, if you take payments.
  • Maintenance and support, if you want ongoing changes covered.

Ask for every one of these to be itemised in your quote before you approve it. A package price on its own does not tell you what the website costs to run.

FAQ

Website development questions.

Do you work with businesses outside Navi Mumbai?
Yes. Netqorix is based in Nerul, Navi Mumbai and serves businesses across Mumbai, India and internationally. Share your location and requirements when you enquire.
Can my website really launch in 10 days?
The 10-day commitment applies to the defined business-website scope when content and approvals are ready. E-commerce, custom applications and additional requirements need a separately agreed delivery plan.
What should I prepare before development starts?
Prepare your business description, service list, contact details, logo and any photos or project examples you want to use. We confirm who supplies each piece of content and any required account access during scoping.
Will the website rank first on Google?
No search position is guaranteed. The Growth package includes SEO setup and analytics. Your visibility also depends on the searches you target, competition and the usefulness of your content. We confirm the specific SEO deliverables in your scope.
Who owns the website after launch?
You own the approved source code and intellectual property after final settlement. You can maintain the site yourself, use another provider or discuss optional support with Netqorix.
Are hosting and maintenance included?
Inclusions and recurring charges depend on the agreed setup. Ask for domain, hosting, email, paid tools and maintenance to be itemised before approving your quote. Optional ongoing support is available separately.
What is the difference between a website and a web application?
A website presents information: visitors read it and then contact you. A web application lets people do something — log in, book, pay, track or manage records. If you need the second, see mobile and web app development, which is priced differently because it is software rather than pages.
Do I need an interactive 3D website?
Usually not. Interactive 3D earns its cost for products where configuration or internal detail drives the sale. For a standard services business, a well-built Growth site will generate more enquiries for far less money, and that is the honest recommendation.
Can I update the website myself after launch?
With the Growth package, yes — it includes a CMS, which is an admin screen where you change text, images and prices without a developer. The Starter package does not include a CMS; small changes can be handled through a maintenance plan or quoted individually.
