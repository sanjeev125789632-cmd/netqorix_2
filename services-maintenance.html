05 / GOVERNANCE & POLICIES

Frequently Asked Questions: Retainers, SLAs & Governance

Clear operational answers on retainer utilization, emergency escalation, legacy adoption, and code ownership.

Do unused retainer hours roll over to the next month?
Yes. Under our Growth Retainer plan, up to 50% of unused hours automatically roll over into the immediately following month. This provides flexibility during lighter operational cycles while ensuring dedicated engineering availability remains balanced and predictable. Basic Retainer hours are designed for active ongoing maintenance and reset monthly to maintain guaranteed bandwidth.
How does our team submit and escalate emergency P1 tickets?
Retainer clients receive access to our dedicated Emergency WhatsApp on-call line, priority phone hotline, and private Slack/Discord channel. In addition, our automated synthetic monitors alert our engineers 24/7 the moment an outage occurs—often before your internal team or customers notice. P1 critical tickets bypass standard ticket queues and immediately trigger SMS/Call alerts to on-duty engineers with a guaranteed sub-15-minute response SLA.
Can Netqorix adopt and maintain an existing legacy codebase built elsewhere?
Yes. Roughly 40% of our retainer clients joined us with codebases originally constructed by third-party agencies or in-house developers who departed. We begin every new partnership with a Day 1–14 Discovery Audit to evaluate test coverage, identify unpatched security vulnerabilities, map infrastructure architecture, and establish runbooks before formalizing binding SLA tiers.
What happens if our hours run out mid-month during an unexpected sprint?
We believe in zero billing surprises. When you reach 80% of your allocated monthly retainer hours, you receive an automated notification. If additional tasks are requested within the same month, we quote them at your pre-agreed discounted retainer hourly rate. No billable work is initiated without your explicit written approval.
Who owns the source code, patches, and configurations created during the retainer?
You retain 100% ownership of all source code, database structures, documentation, and configuration scripts. All work is committed directly to your private git repository (GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket). Netqorix operates strictly on a work-for-hire basis with zero platform lock-in.
What is the minimum contract commitment and cancellation policy?
Our support retainers operate on an initial 3-month commitment (to allow for the deep discovery audit, stabilization, and baseline performance metrics), followed by a straightforward month-to-month agreement. You may cancel at any time with 30 days written notice. No punitive cancellation fees or long-term vendor traps.
