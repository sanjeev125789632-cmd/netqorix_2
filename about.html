04 / HOW WE WORK

Principles you can hold us to.

01

Fixed quote before work begins.

Pages, functions, integrations, content responsibilities and delivery milestones are confirmed first. Additions are priced separately before they are built.

02

100% code and IP ownership.

You receive the approved source code and intellectual property after final settlement, without platform or vendor lock-in.

03

Live in 10 days.

The 10-day commitment applies to the defined business-website scope when content and approvals are ready. Not live in 10 days? You don't pay the balance.

04

Direct access to the founder.

No sales maze. Your requirement goes directly to Sanjeev, with a fixed quote within 24 business hours of your brief.

05

Stated deliverables and exclusions.

Every service page lists what is included and what is not, so there are no surprises.

06

Honest reporting.

Where results depend on outside factors, such as search rankings, no outcome is promised. What is committed to is the work carried out.

05 / THE PROCESS

Four gates to handover.

GATE 01

Scope & Discovery

Goals, pages, users, integrations and approval route are defined.

OUTPUT: Scope document · Fixed quote · Delivery plan
GATE 02

Design & Architecture

Information hierarchy, responsive behavior and technical structure.

OUTPUT: Design system · Page direction · Architecture notes
GATE 03

Build & Integration

Production code, forms, content, integrations and device checks.

OUTPUT: Repository · Working pages · Testing report
GATE 04

Deploy & Handover

DNS, SSL, production release and complete ownership transfer.

OUTPUT: Live website · Credentials · Handover documentation
